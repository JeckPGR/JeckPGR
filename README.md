### 🧭 My Philosophy

I sit at the intersection of **building** and **understanding why it's being built**. Two years across full-stack development, project management, and now Product Management taught me that shipping code is only half the job — the other half is understanding the workflow, the user, and the business constraint behind it.

That's shaped a few things I hold onto:

- **Understand the system before you touch the code.** At Bank Mandiri, before writing a single recommendation, I spent months mapping 11 procurement applications and validating 280+ bank guarantees — because you can't fix what you haven't fully understood.
- **Technical depth keeps product thinking honest.** I'm moving toward Product Management, but I keep building (Inotrive, Yayzi, EduLocal) so my product sense stays grounded in what's actually feasible to ship.
- **Automation is a form of respect for people's time.** Whether it's saving an ops team 12+ hours a week with spreadsheet automation, or centralizing payroll for 30+ F&B employees across 4 outlets — the goal is always to remove friction, not add features.
- **Data over assumptions.** From analyzing 50+ MLBB match replays to using the TELOS framework to evaluate process improvements — I'd rather decide with evidence than instinct.
